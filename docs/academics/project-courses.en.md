# Course Projects, Capstone, and Dissertation Applications

!!! warning "Use current-term official information"
    Capacity, eligibility, topics, approvers, and deadlines change by term. Group chats and shared spreadsheets are leads only; verify the final state in the current official course system and notices.

## Purpose

Describe the general preparation and confirmation process for applying to ME5100, ME5400, Capstone, Dissertation, or other course projects through laboratory research projects.

## Prerequisites

- Confirm the course code, eligibility, credits, timetable, and registration window on the current-term course page.
- Confirm that the research direction matches the course learning objectives.
- Evaluate conflicts among on-site experiments, coursework, and research commitments.
- Prepare a summary of background, relevant skills, interests, and available time.

## Procedure

1. Confirm eligibility in the official course system; a group-chat signup or shared-spreadsheet entry is not enrollment.
2. Select an area from [Research Directions and Project Opportunities](../projects/index.en.md).
3. Contact the relevant **Project Coordinator** through the controlled directory and submit your background and interest.
4. Discuss the task, on-site attendance, deliverables, intellectual property, and data-confidentiality requirements with the owner.
5. If the course requires an approval letter, supervisor approval, or self-proposed-project form, obtain formal confirmation before the current-term deadline.
6. Complete course or project submission in the official system and retain the receipt.

## Suggested Application Materials

- Name and university account information, submitted only through an approved form
- Course code, project direction, and current term
- Relevant coursework, engineering, or research experience
- Available time and on-site experiment availability
- Skills matching the project and expected learning goals

## Verification

- The official system shows the correct course or project status.
- Formal confirmation has been received from the course, supervisor, or project owner.
- No unresolved conflict remains with other courses, work, or on-site experiments.
- Personal information does not appear in the public Wiki, Issues, or repositories.

## Troubleshooting

- Name appears in a shared sheet but not the official system: treat the official system as authoritative and ask the course administrator to investigate.
- Capacity or topic differs from a group-chat message: recheck the current-term course notice.
- Approval letter not received: do not treat verbal interest as approval; contact the owner promptly.
- Course and project schedules conflict: resolve the conflict with both owners before the registration deadline.
- Still interested after unsuccessful registration: use the controlled interest form and wait for the owner to confirm next steps.
