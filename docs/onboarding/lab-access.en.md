# Laboratory Access Application

## Purpose

Apply for access to laboratory `COM2-0106`.

## Prerequisites

- Have an NUS account that can sign in to MySoC.
- Confirm the Supervisor SoC ID with the laboratory administrator.

## Application Form

[Open the access application form](https://mysoc.nus.edu.sg/app/eform/forms/formE.php)

!!! info "Form guidance"
    - After opening the form, provide your personal information, requested access period, and reason for applying as instructed on the page.
    - Confirm the current Supervisor SoC ID with the laboratory administrator.
    - If the page is unavailable off campus, try connecting to the NUS VPN.

## Required Information

- Supervisor SoC ID: `<SUPERVISOR_SOC_ID>`
- Laboratory location: `COM2-0106`

## Procedure

1. Open the application form and enter the information requested on the page.
2. Enter the Supervisor SoC ID and laboratory location shown above.
3. Review the information and submit the application.

## Verification

Confirm that the page shows a successful submission, then follow its instructions to check the application status.

## Troubleshooting

Connect to the campus network or the NUS VPN.
If you still cannot access or submit the form after connecting to the NUS VPN, contact the laboratory administrator.
