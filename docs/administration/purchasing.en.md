# Purchasing Process

This process standardizes group requirements, product selection, weekend consolidation, purchasing in the following week, package collection, and inventory records. It is intended to reduce duplicate purchases, incompatible models, missing accessories, and items that remain unused after purchase.

!!! info "Weekly purchasing schedule"
    Each group submits only one Excel purchase list and one total shared-cart link per week. The complete submission is sent to the Purchasing Coordinator on Saturday or Sunday. The current week's consolidated requests are purchased in the following week, and weekly batches must remain separate.

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Group members | Confirm the need together, compare suppliers, discuss product selection, and complete feasibility research |
| Group Purchasing Submitter | Prepare the group's single weekly Excel file, verify the items and cart, and submit the total shared-cart link |
| Purchasing Coordinator | Consolidate submissions over the weekend and check fields, duplicates, cart consistency, research, and approval status |
| Purchasing Executor | Place orders in the following week by separate weekly batch and put delivered packages in the common area |
| Owning group | Collect and unpack packages, verify and photograph each item, update the corresponding Notion record, and remain responsible for its items |

## Prerequisites

- Group members have jointly confirmed the need and selection; no individual makes the selection alone.
- Every item has a clear purpose, expected users, and expected usage period, and the purchase is genuinely needed.
- Existing inventory has been checked and no suitable item can be reused or shared.
- The group has compared as many suppliers or reasonable alternatives as practical and researched the model, quantity, compatibility, required accessories, and delivery.
- All required accompanying items are included in the same weekly request so that missing accessories are not discovered after delivery.
- Higher-value items have received prior confirmation under the lab's current approval requirements.
- Any item eligible for `SG199` free shipping uses a free-shipping option; a special exception is explained in the Excel file.

## Submission Procedure

1. Define the complete purpose and requirements within the group. Compare at least three suppliers or reasonable alternatives where practical, then confirm the model, quantity, amount, and required accessories.
2. The Group Purchasing Submitter prepares the group's only Excel file for the week. Every item must include its name, quantity, model, amount, purpose, product link, and application group.
3. Add every confirmed item for the week to one cart on the relevant platform. Verify models, quantities, accessories, prices, and shipping, then generate one total shared-cart link.
4. Send the Excel file and total shared-cart link together as one complete submission to the Purchasing Coordinator on Saturday or Sunday. Individual requests sent as text, chat messages, or other scattered formats do not enter the purchasing process.
5. The Purchasing Coordinator consolidates the group submissions over the weekend and checks complete fields, duplicates, Excel-to-cart consistency, feasibility research, shipping, and approval status. Non-compliant requests are returned to the owning group for completion.
6. The current week's consolidated requests are purchased in the following week as a separate batch. The Purchasing Executor must not mix requests from different weeks or substitute a model when availability, price, or specification changes without group confirmation.
7. After ordering, update the actual purchase information in the Ordered Items Table for the corresponding purchase week. Keep each weekly record separate.

## Feasibility and Selection Requirements

Confirm at least the following before submitting each item:

- **Practical use:** The related project, experiment, or maintenance task, expected users, and usage period are clear.
- **Supplier comparison:** As many suppliers or reasonable alternatives as practical have been compared, with a rationale based on function, price, delivery, after-sales support, or maintenance.
- **Compatibility:** The item matches existing equipment, software, interfaces, dimensions, power, materials, and the experimental plan.
- **Completeness:** Quantities, cables, adapters, mounting parts, consumables, and other required accessories have been checked together so that nothing is missing after delivery.
- **Supplier and shipping risk:** The product link, stock, lead time, after-sales terms, and shipping option have been checked. Items eligible for `SG199` free shipping must use free shipping; a special exception must be explained in the Excel file and reviewed.

Higher-value items also require a more thorough comparison of alternatives, verification of critical compatibility, a written usage plan, and prior confirmation under the lab's current approval requirements. The Wiki does not set a separate monetary threshold.

## Delivery, Collection, and Notion Updates

1. The Purchasing Executor puts delivered packages in the common area and retains enough application-group or order information for the owning group to identify them.
2. The owning group collects and unpacks its packages, then checks each item's name, model, quantity, amount correspondence, required accessories, and physical condition.
3. The owning group photographs every item and updates the delivery and photograph record in the Ordered Items Table for the corresponding purchase week.
4. After confirming that the physical items, photographs, and Notion records match, complete inventory registration and move the items to their designated storage location. The owning group remains responsible for its purchases.

For a wrong, missing, damaged, or unusable item, pause inventory registration, retain the packaging and photographs, and contact the Purchasing Coordinator. Do not substitute the item or close the record independently.

## Troubleshooting

- **Individual or multiple submissions from one group:** Do not consolidate them; return them to the group to combine into one weekly Excel file.
- **Missed weekend consolidation or incomplete material:** Complete the file for the next weekly purchasing cycle; do not purchase before completion.
- **Missing purpose, research, accessories, or shipping-exception explanation:** Return the request to the owning group for completion before consolidation.
- **Shared cart cannot be opened or does not match the Excel file:** The owning group generates a new link and verifies the products, models, quantities, and amounts.
- **Item unavailable, price increase, or specification change:** Pause the item and return it to the owning group for confirmation; do not buy a substitute directly.
- **Duplicate request or usable inventory found:** Pause the item and confirm whether it can be shared or reused before purchasing.
- **Wrong, missing, or damaged delivery:** Pause inventory registration, retain photographs of the packaging and item, and ask the Purchasing Coordinator to contact the supplier.
- **Package cannot be identified or Notion cannot be updated:** Leave the item unchanged and ask the Purchasing Coordinator to verify the owning group, weekly batch, and record.
