# Reimbursement

## Prerequisites

- Obtain prior approval
- Retain compliant invoices and proof of payment
- Record the purpose and project for each expense

## Procedure

1. Organize invoices and proof of payment.
2. Complete the reimbursement form.
3. Ask the responsible person to verify it.
4. Submit it to the administrative contact.

## Verification

Confirm that a submission receipt was received and record the expected processing status.
